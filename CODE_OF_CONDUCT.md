# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment
We are committed to making participation in Bizone open, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected Behavior
1. Be respectful and professional.
2. Give and accept constructive feedback.
3. Focus on the technical issue, not the person.
4. Use inclusive language.

## Unacceptable Behavior
1. Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
2. Discriminatory language or conduct.
3. Public or private intimidation.
4. Sharing private information without permission.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributors that violate this policy.

## Reporting
Report incidents to: `support@bizone.trade`.
